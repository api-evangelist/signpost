# Signpost

> Signpost provides AI-powered and live virtual receptionist / answering services for small businesses, with a focus on home-services contractors (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing). It captures and responds to inbound calls, texts, and messages across phone, text, Angi, Thumbtack, and Facebook, unifies them in a single messaging inbox, and integrates with CRM/scheduling systems. Signpost is backed by GV (Google Ventures). It publishes no public developer API surface as of this profile.
